# Confusing secrets / rare traps

2026-10-17. Background stuff. Not everywhere. Not combat roster. Not new Resolve.

Rare, confusing, or mean little things that exist in the world and do not announce themselves.

White Knuckle supplies some tonal inspiration for a few of these (and for certain creatures), but the implementations are significantly different. These are not ports, not anomalous objects, and not the same rules.

---

## Explosive doors

Rare trap. The White Knuckle cursed door is a loose reference point only. This is not an anomalous door.

It is a conventional door, trapped with an explosive charge that detonates when opened. Looks like a normal door with one of those bars over it.

- Opens normally if you use the bar / handle the expected way. Charge is on the door or frame; opening it closes the circuit or pulls the pin.
- 1/10,000 chance the door spontaneously opens when a player views it. If the player is within 10 meters but not within 1 meter, it detonates.
- Ordinary explosive packet. No new status. No glory. Just a door that sometimes tries to kill you for looking at it from a medium distance.
- Extremely rare. Background. Not a puzzle. Not labeled. If you see one detonate on its own, that is the whole joke.

## Developer entity (secret joke encounter)

Secret non-hostile civilian encounter. The developer as a person in the level. She knows what Ryko is and gets nervous around her. Not a combat roster entry. Not a boss. Dies to ordinary packets.

### Sheet (vs operator human-meat baseline with cyber+chems)

Operators are at least 3× stronger (super soldiers). Raw civilian numbers:

| Field | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Mass | 70 kg bare / ~82 kg kit / ~88 kg heavy armor | 155 lbs bare. |
| Strength | ~−65% to −70% vs operator | One-hand lift max ~30 kg. Carry strength 225 lbs (~102 kg). Decent for a civilian, still far under operator cyber. |
| Agility | ~−10% to 0% | Slight edge from gaming + minor martial arts. Can slip an average person; a trained operator is out of the question. |
| Speed | ~−5% to 0% | Slightly quicker than pure average. |
| Stamina | ~−10% | Odd jobs, not operator endurance. |
| HP | ~−30% | Civilian, no baked cyber. |
| Plate | none by default; medium personnel armor if she loots a rack | |
| Starting weapon | small pocketknife (decently proficient) | |

### Behavior

- Non-hostile by default.
- Gets nervous in Ryko’s presence (knows exactly what she is).
- If guns fire within earshot / line of sight in the past five minutes: rushes to break into an equipment rack for medium personnel armor, or hides and steals gear.
- Otherwise tries to stay out of the way.

### Perks (joke AI stamps, not shop)

- **Advanced tactical planning** — AI can mentally plan ahead, queue actions, and has corner-checking prerequisites.
- **Fourth wall breaker** — AI can animation-cancel, use minor glitches, and abuse in-game system math. Flavor: “I think I know how this game works.”
- **First principles fighter** — AI rapidly learns proficiency with weapons in the area. Faster reload and improved handling over time. Does not meaningfully improve accuracy. Flavor: “The mechanism below actions is what you see instead of the action. You will assimilate skill, but never truly have the full extent and inexpressable information.”

### Debuffs

- **Terror** — same fear response as enemies.
- **Poor training** — reduced accuracy, increased fear, easier to fool, increased focus time on guns. Cannot hit shots reliably without ADS.

The joke is that she understands the systems better than the soldiers and still has civilian numbers. She plans, glitches, and learns weapons quickly, then misses because she is not an operator.
