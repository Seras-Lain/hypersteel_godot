# Silly creatures / world entities

2026-10-16. Not combat roster. Not new Resolve. Passive or one-shot joke entities.

These are typically non-combatant creatures that inhabit the world, often as set dressing. Defined as non-humanoid entities made of tissue.

**Shared rules for the small tissue ones:**
- Interact prompt allows petting. Occasional hum sound. Hum louder if placed near trees.
- +1 HP if killed. +2 HP if placed near trees or a pond. Trivial either way; you can be nice.
- Cannot be locked onto by smartgun (small size + players would feel bad).
- No new Resolve. Ordinary packets if they somehow take damage. No glory.

## Hydrocarbon slime

Undifferentiated multicellular organism which eats hydrocarbons. Non-combatant. Found around oil spills.

Yellow slime-mold-like creature of forgotten origin. Consumes oil or medium-chain liquid hydrocarbons as food, converting them to energy. When distressed, produces hydrogen gas as a defense mechanism and plastic threads to reinforce itself or hold to a surface.

**Passive entity.**

### Biology (diegetic)

- Semi-symbiotic system of specialized protozoa, bacteria, and fungi, similar to a lichen but with slime mold.
- All cells from the same base template; may differentiate.
- Simple set of brains located in a **core**: spherical node 1–3 cm, several connection holes, shields the brain against impact force. Intelligence correlated to how many cores it has.
- Senses: smell, temperature, taste. Various cells act as eye-dots that sense infrared light at different levels. No conventional vision.
- Massive energy density. Can hibernate or use stored energy to emit heat as needed, for hundreds of years in a temperate climate.
- Does **not** have runaway exponential growth even with functionally infinite food. One will pace itself while reproducing asexually. Capable of horizontal gene transfer.

### Behavior / numbers

- Avoids gunfire. Terrified of fire.
- +400% weakness to fire. −80% damage taken if shot. 90% resistance to impact damage.
- Burn when killed. No health gained from killing them. Why would you do this.
- If thrown, attempts to form hydrogen bubbles inside itself so it does not splatter on impact. In balloon shape (often during digestion or under physical threat) it is vulnerable to fire.
- Can change consistency at will: ball (carryable) or ooze.
- May be carried, but only if the player looks at them while holding a bucket.
- Carryable in a backpack, belt pack, or one hand once obtained that way.
- Can be placed in enemy vehicles to get them to make any FUEL-operated vehicle have reduced fuel in the tank after enough time, and potentially occupy soldiers inside.
- Can be “pet” or fed, but for no benefit. Will swirl about, unsure of what’s going on.

### Game use

- Can be placed inside the gas tank of a vehicle that uses gasoline. It drinks the fuel and oozes out the gas cap, taking the gasoline with it. Vehicle will not start.
- Not hostile. Does not attack.
- No new damage packets. Fire vulnerability while ballooned is ordinary Burning / heat. Hydrogen is just flavor + the balloon state.

## Big roaches

10 cm roaches that fly if startled.

Some of them have small quantities of titanium in their shells due to genetic engineering making them concentrate trace minerals. Crushing them drops metal scrap, helping restore robot health.

## Isopods

Amphibious isopods that often eat non-guilded roaches or leaf litter.

**White isopod:** same as before, but with a calcified shell.

### Full isopod (15 cm)

- Avoids loud noises and eats leaves. Gang up on anything very weak or some plants, eating it.
- Attracted to music.
- Occasionally attempts to eat dead bodies. They don’t like how they taste.
- Avoids anything moving. Docile scavengers. Walks on all surfaces.
- Roll into balls or hide if frightened. Make noise to each other if frightened.
- Can be thrown into an enemy, blinding if the face is hit. This will result in nearby isopods being hostile for the next 2 hours (even if the game is closed) or until respawn.
- Secret joke: 3 isopods can fire a normal pistol as a crewed weapon from a rack or fallen enemy.
- Do not react negatively if carried and set down. May curl up if picked up.
- Found near ponds or trees. Social, found in small groups.
- Can be fed roaches or other small bugs or any plant.

## Birds

Birds are technically background particles, but come with a hitbox. Firing at them kills them. There is no gain to this.

Actual birds. Two animations. Fly away when you get close. Can be shot. Drop 1 health when meleed.

## Cat (undercity / one section)

Just kinda chilling in exactly one section. You may pet it, after letting it sniff your finger.

- +1 HP/s if pet (because morale).
- +10 HP if killed. Why would you do this.
- Chases weapon laser dots.

## Guard dog

Found occasionally in human settlements.

- Actually may bite the player to little effect due to armor.
- May be pet if not attacking and not approached at high speed (startled).
- Enraged if handler or ally killed in sight, and run away if thrown.
- Always finds the player within 10 meters at all times.

Modes: sleep, wake, attack bite, attack hold player down (as with most melee, air dash + melee to break), search, pet, eating.

Afraid of explosives or shockwaves.

## Rat

- Runs away if seen.
- Dies if stepped on.
- RNG-based chance of a bite attack which can’t penetrate the player’s boot to damage them. Makes funny noises. Chased by cats.

---

## Microraptor / minidragon

Exactly what it sounds like. No fire breathing. Bird-size flying creature that avoids players. Really just there for set decoration. May attempt to catch rats or small birds.

---

## Fish

Found in lakes. Killing them results in the fish floating to the surface of the water. +3 HP pickup × size (typically ~0.2). Seemingly dressing for the lakes.

---

## Leaping crab

Looks a little like a horseshoe crab, but will leap at entities that startle it.

---

## Spooder

A spider with long legs, but it eats medium to small bugs. They have two arms and GRAB small creatures, and six legs. Similar to the real-life tailless whip spider, but bigger.

---

## Flybug

A fly-like bug that spooders or the like like to eat. They are a little annoying.

---

## Moth

It’s a big bird-size moth. It can emit light like a firefly. Likes the very large flowers of mega-vines. Of rat-like intelligence.

## Joke entity: Slenderman pages (red forests, before Gobi)

In the red forests before the Gobi level, pages are scattered across the level.

- Collecting all of them triggers a short section where a robot in the shape of Slenderman becomes aggressive and fights you.
- The Slenderman does **not** kill. It nails you to the floor with a spike through your hand, making melee or shooting worse until Ryko fixes it on screen.
- Dialogue: “that’s my vent art give it back” before leaving.
- It stops being hostile if the pages are dropped out of your inventory.
- Pages take up no inventory slots and cannot be removed until this point.

One-shot joke. Not a real enemy roster entry. No glory kill, no new Resolve. Spike is a temporary status / grip that Ryko clears in a short on-screen beat.
